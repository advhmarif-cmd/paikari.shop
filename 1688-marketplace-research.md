## Findings used for Paikari planning

1688-style marketplaces are primarily wholesale sourcing platforms rather than ordinary retail catalogs. The important buyer experience is broad product discovery, supplier/store context, bulk pricing, minimum order quantities, and the ability to compare sourcing options.

The WorldFirst guide describes factory and wholesaler sellers, quantity-dependent pricing, generally lower MOQs than traditional export channels, supplier business information, direct supplier communication, price negotiation, and the importance of freight-forwarding/logistics workflows for international sourcing. It also notes that overseas buyers may need an agent or intermediary for cross-border purchasing.

For Paikari.shop in Bangladesh, the equivalent product should therefore support B2B buyer identity, supplier profiles, MOQ-aware quantity selection, wholesale price tiers, vendor product ownership, supplier contact/inquiry, local delivery and payment workflows, and clear separation between B2C retail purchasing and B2B bulk purchasing. Cross-border payment, international freight forwarding, factory verification, and full negotiation automation should be treated as later phases unless the business model explicitly requires them.
